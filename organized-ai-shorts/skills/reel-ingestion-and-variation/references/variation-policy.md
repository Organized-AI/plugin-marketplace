# Originality and controlled variance

The target is format fluency, not identity duplication.

## Preserve only at the pattern level

- format grammar: comparison, ranked list, demo, repo scan, before/after, or explainer;
- functional beats: hook, evidence, mechanism, builder value, payoff, CTA;
- approximate pacing and mobile-first information density;
- the user’s own topic or authorized source material.

## Change materially

- script wording, examples, claims, and CTA;
- screenshots, footage, diagrams, icons, audio, and voice;
- typography, palette, composition, transitions, and motion language;
- shot order, timing, and editorial emphasis;
- proof points and brand markers.
